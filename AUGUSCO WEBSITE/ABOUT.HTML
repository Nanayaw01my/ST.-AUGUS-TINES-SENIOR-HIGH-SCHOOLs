About Us - St. Augustine Senior High School

About St. Augustine Senior High School

For over five decades, we have been committed to providing quality education that transforms lives and builds future leaders.

Our Mission

To provide holistic education that develops students academically, morally, and socially, empowering them to become responsible citizens and future leaders who contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be a center of academic excellence that nurtures well-rounded individuals equipped with knowledge, skills, and values to excel in higher education and make meaningful contributions to national development.

Our Rich History

St. Augustine Senior High School was established in 1965 with the mission of providing quality secondary education to the youth of Ghana. Founded by the Catholic Mission, the school began with just 50 students and 5 teachers.

Over the years, we have grown significantly, now serving over 1,200 students with a dedicated staff of 75 teachers. Our campus has expanded to include modern science laboratories, a library, computer labs, and excellent sports facilities.

Throughout our history, we have maintained a tradition of academic excellence, with our students consistently performing well in the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE).
