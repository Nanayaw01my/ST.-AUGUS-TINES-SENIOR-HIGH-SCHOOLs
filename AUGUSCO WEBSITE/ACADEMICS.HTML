Our Academic Programs

St. Augustine Senior High School offers a diverse range of programs designed to prepare students for higher education and successful careers.

General Science

Our General Science program provides a strong foundation in physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics, preparing students for careers in medicine, engineering, and research.

  • Advanced laboratory facilities
  • Science and math competitions
  • Research projects
  • University preparation
Learn More

Business

Our Business program equips students with essential skills in accounting, economics, and entrepreneurship, preparing them for careers in business management and finance.

  • Entrepreneurship projects
  • Financial literacy training
  • Business competitions
  • Internship opportunities
Learn More

General Arts

Our General Arts program offers a diverse curriculum including literature, history, geography, and government, preparing students for careers in law, education, and public service.

  • Creative writing workshops
  • Debate and public speaking
  • Cultural studies
  • Research and analysis skills
Learn More

Home Economics

Our Home Economics program focuses on food and nutrition, clothing and textiles, and management, preparing students for careers in hospitality, fashion, and family studies.

  • Modern kitchen facilities
  • Fashion design projects
  • Nutrition education
  • Hospitality training
Learn More

Visual Arts

Our Visual Arts program nurtures creativity through drawing, painting, sculpture, and graphic design, preparing students for careers in fine arts, design, and multimedia.

  • Studio art facilities
  • Art exhibitions
  • Digital art training
  • Portfolio development
Learn More

Agricultural Science

Our Agricultural Science program combines theory with practical farming skills, preparing students for careers in agribusiness, environmental science, and food production.

  • School farm
  • Agribusiness projects
  • Environmental studies
  • Modern farming techniques
